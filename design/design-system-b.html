Design system

An EduApp-inspired system for study seats: white surfaces, charcoal text, strong condensed headings, blue actions and quiet gray navigation. Familiar, compact and clear.

Option B preserves every original section and specimen. The shared components receive one consistent visual treatment, inspired by the EduApp screenshots you supplied.

Use a condensed sans-serif with regular body text and bold headings. This offline mockup prefers locally installed Bahnschrift, Roboto Condensed or Arial Narrow, then system fallbacks. The exact EduApp font is unverified; no fonts are downloaded.

Shape and spacing

Card radius 7
481216243248

Cards use a 7 px radius, controls 5 px and dialogs 8 px. Use 1 px gray borders and a light shadow on raised surfaces. Selected navigation is gray; active tabs are white on a gray strip. Blue focus rings make interactive controls clear. Keep the original spacing scale.

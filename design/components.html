Popups

Two kinds only. Try them: a decision sheet slides up from the bottom and a notification banner slides in from the top. Closing a sheet any way except its right-hand button counts as Cancel.

Decision sheet

Two equal buttons. Left is the safe option, right is the action (red outline when risky). Labels are verbs.

Notification banner

Urgent ones (check-in reminder, seat lost, session end) stay until dismissed.

Camera view

Dark in both themes, because it is a camera. The scan line animates with a calm loop and stops when the phone has reduce-motion on.

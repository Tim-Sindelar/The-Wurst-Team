# Quick close after the live check-in

Start when the teammate is seated and the judges have seen the session timer. Click the live image, press Right / Space, or use P with the presentation focused. For an external video window, return to the pitch browser and press P during the switch. The slides cut through black and wait for the next click; the film's automatic pacing does not apply here.

The first slide begins with QR check-in only. Each click / Right / Space reveals the next line and swaps the static render. After the coffee mug, the next click goes to Study together. Left reverses the reveals.

On the adoption slide, click / Right / Space advances the three zoom levels. The admin panel appears on a 3D laptop only at the bold Entire ETH step; it hides at the library and other-universities steps. Left or right-click reverses each level. From the first level it returns to Built for ETH; from the final level, forward continues to Q&A. Backward from Q&A restores the widest adoption view. Left or right-click also goes back through the other slides and feature reveals; from the first QR feature it returns to the live handoff.

The final Q&A scene keeps gentle, varied movement and never restarts automatically. Reduced motion freezes the campus. Finish speaking and leave it on screen for questions. Left returns to the previous slide; R restarts the presentation. E jumps to the final scene after the live handoff.

The app opens Home in a phone at the top right of Q&A, restoring the saved booking from the demo. It currently uses the local backend with the imported production catalogue and synthetic occupancy. Use it directly for follow-up questions. A small non-interactive admin laptop at the bottom left invites questions about room setup; keep the hosted `/admin` website ready on another desktop for an interactive explanation. Click the surrounding campus to focus the presentation, then press D to enlarge the phone in the centre; D again returns it to the corner. Clicking the phone's outer frame also toggles its size. The screen itself remains interactive. Enlarging it hides the static admin laptop and its label.

The feature phones are bundled screenshots of the actual app UI from an isolated sample-data preview. No booking, break, extension or end-session request was submitted. The scanner uses the actual ScanScreen with a simulated QR camera feed for the screenshot; it did not access a physical camera. The handover feature shows the actual end-session confirmation, which frees the seat. The friends phone uses the actual planner UI with four sample assignments and no reservation submitted. The portrait map capture shows HG in the app's 3D Workspaces mode. The admin image is the app's built-in admin layout editor, showing furniture tools and a sample room with sixteen seats. No workspace was edited for the capture. The QR illustration is not a working seat token. The final campus and adoption models are imagined outcomes. Free access is the intended policy. The university rollout is a proposal; other universities have not been integrated. Department permissions and library enforcement are proposed pilot work; do not describe them as already implemented or agreed. Campus room bookings are not occupancy sensors.
